Get your education benefits

Florida residency: Your parent has been a Florida resident since August 2024. Complete the college's dependent-student residency declaration and submit the requested proof of your parent's Florida domicile.

Chapter 35: If you have not applied, submit VA Form 22-5490 for Chapter 35 Dependents' Educational Assistance. Save the VA eligibility decision. Once you register, ask the college's veterans certifying official to certify your enrollment. VA generally pays Chapter 35 directly to you, not the school.

CSDDV: Submit the Florida Financial Aid Application (FFAA) for the Children and Spouses of Deceased or Disabled Veterans scholarship (CSDDV). Provide the required proof of relationship, veteran disability, and Florida residency. Track the state's application and award deadlines.
