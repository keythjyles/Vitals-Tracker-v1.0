Questions to resolve before committing

Can I attend KSU entirely online from Florida?

Potentially. KSU has online offerings, but the exact degree, course availability, campus requirements, and tuition classification must be confirmed by KSU.

Will one year in Florida automatically make me an in-state student?

No. Florida generally requires evidence of 12 months of bona fide domicile, and dependent-student rules can change whose residence controls. Ask for an official determination.

Will all my KSU credits transfer?

Not necessarily. A Florida institution may accept credits without applying all of them to your chosen major. Obtain a program-specific transfer evaluation before enrollment.

Do I have to decide now where to graduate?

No. The purpose of this plan is to keep your academic progress moving while preserving the ability to choose later.

The point

At 20, you have time to make a smart choice. You don't need to stop college, move back to Georgia, or rush into a Florida transfer just because your circumstances are changing. You can keep learning while you decide where you want to build your life.
