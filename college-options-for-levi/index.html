Residency and benefits

Florida residency: Your parent has lived in Florida since August 2024. Request dependent-student resident tuition classification using your parent's Florida domicile and supporting documents. You do not need to wait another year for your parent to establish residency.

Chapter 35: Apply for or provide your VA Certificate of Eligibility, then contact the college's veterans certifying official after registering.

CSDDV: Apply for Florida's Children and Spouses of Deceased or Disabled Veterans scholarship. Confirm award eligibility and applicable academic year with the state and college.
