Video Sound Production Utilities

Video editing

  • CapLockOpen a video file. Place captions, animations and sound as editable layers.
  • ClipperCut videos with sound or make numbered, transparent caption clips.
  • FrameLockStep and trim by source frame. Mark corrections and take frame-specific notes.
  • AspectLockFrame a vertical scene and align an animation over a video bed.
  • MotionLockMark camera cuts, zoom levels and B-roll for a motion plan.

Sound editing

  • SxLockPlace sound effects, mix tracks and record voice against video.
  • Sound ScrubberFind, audition and approve sounds for the effects library.
  • WavLockMark and trim audio precisely. Save reusable WAV clips.

  • JokeLabDevelop joke text, variants and production prompts.
  • Jokelet WizardBuild an individual jokelet from voice, performance and reaction.
  • Jokelet AssemblyPut jokelets, B-roll and an outro into an episode.
  • PreviewLockOpen and review video from the saved cloud library.
  • Finished video reviewReview and approve finished videos for release.
  • Media LibraryBrowse reactions, performances, audio and other reusable assets.
  • GS Field GuideCheck FG-01 caption timing and the Gemini test status.
