SxLock information

Live recording and mixing

Tap Rec to start recording your microphone. No video is required. Without a video, the take is saved to My sounds for later placement. With a ready video before its end, playback starts from the current position and the take is also inserted there. The red button and RECORDING indicator flash during capture. Stop finishes the take; Take downloads its original audio.

Live audio stays separate. After recording, open Mixer to balance Video audio, Effects and Live recordings. Each insertion also has its own volume, timing, trim and loop controls. Mixer levels apply to playback and video/WAV exports; the raw Take download stays unchanged. Headphones prevent the soundtrack entering your microphone.

Video audio includes any music and ambience already baked into the source. Those parts cannot be separated by this mixer. Imported sounds use the Effects level; microphone takes use Live recordings.

Recording library

Manage my recordings lists imported audio and microphone takes alphabetically. Delete forever requires a warning confirmation and also removes that recording’s insertions in this project. It deletes this browser’s stored copy, not original files, downloads, Drive files or previously saved project backups. Built-in effects remain available.

Working and saving

Rec starts microphone capture, with or without a video. Headphones prevent the soundtrack entering your microphone. Each take becomes an editable sound.

Tap: pause · Hold: effects menu

This includes any music and ambience already in your video.

Every committed edit autosaves in this browser. The original video and your audio are retained for automatic recovery. Wait for Saved locally before closing. Storage failures are shown above the controls. Browser data can be cleared or evicted, so keep original media and project backups in Drive. Project JSON includes your audio and settings; its video reference restores on this browser. On another device, select the original video once.

Recordings are kept in your browser and included in portable project saves.

Tap an insertion to edit. One-shots and timed loops can overlap.

Video export runs in real time. Keep this page visible and your phone awake until complete. MP4 is offered when this browser supports it; otherwise the available format is WebM.

SxLock is a general-purpose sound-effects tool. Microphone access is requested only when you tap Rec. No account, upload, camera, generation API, or subscription is required.

Loops repeat from Source IN to the end of this recording.

Video-synced takes stop when playback stops. All takes stop when the page is hidden. Finish and save your take before leaving. A take is limited to ten minutes or approximately 32 MB to protect phone memory. Browser support determines available recording formats.
