# Grill Sergeant — singles first, collections later

Owner direction, October 28, 2026. This supersedes episode-first production planning. Existing completed episodes remain reusable sources, with their original masters preserved.

Each joke is the primary production and release unit: its own immediate opening, complete setup/payoff, preserved nonverbal reaction/engineered silence, finished ambience/sound and separate editable caption/caplet layers. No episode introduction is required for a single. Produce, approve and release singles independently. Maintain the authorized three-post daily cadence using finished material; never empty an existing scheduled slot without a replacement.

Collections are a later assembly of existing approved singles. Group by premise, tone or demonstrated audience response; retain each single's identity, original layers, release IDs and measurements. A collection does not authorize regeneration of a voice, face, reaction or caption layer. Do not require five new jokes before any one joke can publish.

Track each release's platform/post ID, exposure, watch time, completion, rewatch evidence when supplied, shares/comments and follows. Compare at similar publication ages and keep paid distribution separate. Use those observations to choose collection membership. Do not declare that singles guarantee views, that a zero-view post proves the joke failed, or that short-form viewers will automatically watch a collection.

The Gemini handback must support one single at a time, including ElevenLabs wording/cues, HeyGen identity/performance and independently editable custom transparent caplets.
