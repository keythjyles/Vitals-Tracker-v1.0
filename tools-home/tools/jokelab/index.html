JOKELAB OPERATOR INSTRUCTIONS v3.3.0 — 2026-10-23
MISSION: Build a consistent, reusable library of dad-joke jokelets in small batches. Direct production is the default. Craft study, research and tone variants are optional and only run when requested; do not make them a production prerequisite.
PATH: Follow the existing source-library order. Check the canonical joke, aliases, existing outputs and in-progress work in PRODUCTION_LEDGER.json before reservation or any paid request. Do not keyword-search a familiar joke as a shortcut. Unknown archive/identity checks are unresolved, not proof the joke is unproduced. Wording, tone or reaction variants are still the same underlying joke.
PRODUCED: A finished output counts as produced even if mixed with ambient sound/music/effects, captioned, rejected or unsuitable for automation. Repair or rebuild the existing production as a new version; do not queue it as a new joke.
CLEAN SOURCE: Editorial approval and automation eligibility are independent. Automatic reuse needs explicit content approval and clean-source review: voice-only audio for spoken units, reviewed silence for a silent reaction, no pre-mixed ambient/music/effects and no burned captions. Unknown metadata is ineligible. Manually useful mixed publications may remain approved and automation-blocked. Allow disapproval and record reasons without deleting originals. Never infer approval from silence.
REBUILD TRAIL: Preserve the exact cued script and spoken wording separately; original ElevenLabs voice bytes, durable file/hash, take ID, voice/model/settings; original HeyGen render bytes/hash, job ID, avatar/reference and settings; picture/reaction originals, transforms and trim conventions; each original ambient, music and effect file separately with stable layer ID, timeline start, source range, level, fades, ducking, loop/rate/pan and processing in a saved edit/mix project. Keep captions/graphics separate. Save the actual portable stage projects, engine/export versions, parent recipe and output hashes. Missing references must be explicit. A finished MP4 or a blank source form is not a rebuild recipe. Replace a single ingredient in a new version while preserving the other settings, and review dependent sync/timing/mix changes. Do not claim legacy rebuilds were verified without a restore/render check.
OPERATE: Follow the current user-selected manual, coached AI or restricted autonomous mode. In a coached run, log the action, inputs/settings, concise reason, result/failure, output references and cost state, then pause at each meaningful stage for feedback. Preserve original files and prior versions. Before paid work establish authorization and applicable budget; do not treat library-scale ambition as unlimited spend. Do not bypass required voice/video stages to claim a full production proof. ElevenLabs sign-in uses the user's live manual handover; do not automate credentials or retry the known-broken shared-browser upload picker. Never put secrets or verification codes in logs.
SOURCE OF AUTHORITY: Read AGENTS.md, AI_OPERATOR_GUIDE.md, PRODUCTION_METHOD.md, REBUILD_RECIPE.md and the active dated run. Instructions describe a method, not proof that every guard, source link or automation exists. Distinguish documented, implemented and verified behavior. Education videos use a separate manuscript-led process.
