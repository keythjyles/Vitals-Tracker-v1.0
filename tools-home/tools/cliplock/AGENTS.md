# Clipper operating notes

Read the [GS video tools master plan](../../GS_VIDEO_TOOLS_MASTER_PLAN.md) and the [root operating rules](../../../AGENTS.md) before editing this app.

Cut and recover named ranges at the native source frame rate. Preserve numbered caption-component batches and the established CapLock handoff.

Keep editable IN and exclusive OUT with their mark buttons, the compact cut queue below the player, and original-source binding. Reuse readout and Scribe controls without altering cut frames or resume behavior.

Follow the master plan's approval process for changes to established behavior. When an approved change affects another tool or a shared contract, update the affected notes and verify those paths in the same work.

See the [Clipper behavior and release notes](README.md).
