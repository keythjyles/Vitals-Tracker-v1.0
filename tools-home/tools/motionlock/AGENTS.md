# MotionLock operating notes

Read the [GS video tools master plan](../../GS_VIDEO_TOOLS_MASTER_PLAN.md) and the [root operating rules](../../../AGENTS.md) before editing this app.

Plan camera motion and B-roll changes against an identified source. Preserve its motion timeline, framing and handoff recipe.

Reuse the shared video readout and Scribe as player controls. Keep a view adjustment separate from a recorded camera move, and keep the native motion workspace available.

Follow the master plan's approval process for changes to established behavior. When an approved change affects another tool or a shared contract, update the affected notes and verify those paths in the same work.

See the [tool registry](../../TOOL_REGISTRY.md).
