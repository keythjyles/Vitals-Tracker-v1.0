Drag to place the source. Rotation must be on before a second finger can scale or rotate the unlocked stage.

Push / Pull

100% keeps the opening size. 150% pushes in; 70% pulls out.

Rotation

Turn Rotation on to enable programmed and second-finger rotation. Snap to square catches angles within 5° of each 90° position; switch it off for free rotation. Finger separation never changes magnification during Action.

Action duration and performance speed

Action continues after individual movements finish and when your fingers leave the screen. Pause before navigating.

Save, open and export

Saves download editable instructions. Keep the original video separately. Paths use normalized viewport coordinates for reuse.

Render applies your saved path to the original frames at normal speed.

Camera controls

Wizard guides opening composition, effect and duration, then arms the viewport. Each settings button opens a popup card. The first finger directs the camera: move down and the picture moves up. With Rotation on, a second finger rotates around the first. Lift either or both fingers freely; Action continues. If the primary finger lifts, the remaining finger becomes the direction controller without a jump.

Pause, navigate to a correction point, then start Action to replace only the path from that time onward. Navigation preserves the path. Raw movement uses linear interpolation with no smoothing.

Play toggles normal review; REV toggles frame-by-frame reverse. Frame buttons step one frame, or 25 on a double tap. The player docks flush to the top. Pull its top handle down to reveal the menu, including Dock player and Unlock player. Pull upward to close the menu. Camera touches do not open it.
