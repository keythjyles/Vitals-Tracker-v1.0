Sound Scrubber R1

Separate research tool. SxLock remains locked. Search Wikimedia Commons for real freely licensed audio; audition, inspect source and download selected recordings.

Search scope & licensing

Results are sourced from Wikimedia Commons and checked against each file's declared license. Only audio formats are displayed. Commons metadata may be incomplete; verify attribution and restrictions before publication. No audio is imported into SxLock automatically.

Choose a category or enter a search.

Tip: Download selected audio, then import through SxLock's existing audio-file picker. This tool does not modify your projects.
