Sound Scrubber GS-TOOL-SOUND-SCRUBBER · R7

Separate research tool. SxLock remains locked. Search Wikimedia Commons for real freely licensed audio; audition, inspect source and download selected recordings.

Search scope & licensing

Results are sourced from Wikimedia Commons and checked against each file's declared license. Only audio formats are displayed. Commons metadata may be incomplete; License and attribution are informational fields; verify usage rights before publication. No audio is imported into SxLock automatically.

Review queue · 0

Licensing is recorded as metadata, never an intake or review gate. Every sound requires an explicit decision. Approve / Hold / Cull. Cull is reversible. Decisions and trim points are saved in this browser; original audio is not deleted.
