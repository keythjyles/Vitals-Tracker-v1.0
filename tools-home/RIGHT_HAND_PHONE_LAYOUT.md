# Right-handed phone layout rule

Established with Keyth on 2026-10-26. Applies to all Thicket production tools, including SxLock, AspectLock, PreviewLock, FrameLock, and future tools.

- The primary user works on a phone 100% of the time and is right-handed.
- Put interactive controls to the RIGHT of the picture or BELOW it. Do not put controls to the left of the picture or in a top toolbar.
- Prioritize repeated actions: forward/back one frame, play/pause, seek, and recording. Keep them in easy right-thumb reach and outside the picture.
- Keep the player's upper edge near the top. A top-docked picture does not justify top-docked controls.
- Put collapse/expand controls below the picture on the right.
- Never require the user's hand to cross or obscure the picture for repeated taps.
- Use at least 44px tap targets. Compact spacing must not mean tiny targets.
- Preserve export framing, source audio, saved projects, media, and existing tool functionality when changing the layout.
- Check narrow portrait-phone layout first; desktop is secondary.

Interpretation of the correction: NEVER LEFT OR TOP controls; ALWAYS RIGHT OR BOTTOM.
